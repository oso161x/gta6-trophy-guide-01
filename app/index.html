Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 and puts the platinum at about 200 hours; Push Square says 250 or more. Every trophy is single-player and offline — there are no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies and stop the game saving, so keep them off the file you use for this run. There is no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave).

  1. Step 1 — Story & optional missions about 60–80 hours

    Play the story at your own pace through five chapters. Along the way: do the Chapter 1 morning meditation at Jason's house (see Positive Mental Attitude — the one trophy that may be missable); take optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of them); scan every new vehicle with WAiNK (100% needs 200 of 277); study new animals through binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255); turn on progress and criminal-profile notifications so you can see what counts.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist & challenges about 100 hours

    After the story, the checklist opens at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Progress from the story already counts and nothing on it is missable. It is lenient — closer to 60% of true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 stunts; robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything; 15 of 18 card sets. This step is where A Long Road, the gold for 100%, is earned.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks & cleanup about 10 hours

    Most trophies should be done by now. Strangers & Freaks is not part of 100%, so finish your 8 stranger strands, clean up anything left on the list, and the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — follows.

Missable? Only one trophy may be: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot). The Chapter 1 balcony meditation can pass you by — do it when it comes up. Every other trophy can be earned after the story. Use the below to see it on its own.

Where the facts come from

Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them, from the frozen official list (18 November 2026) — Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every entry. Every guide on this page is written in the maker's own words and cites its sources beside it: the how-to detail draws on PowerPyx's trophy guide & roadmap, PowerPyx's 100% completion checklist, GTABase's trophy list and sixshore's trophy list & roadmap. Nothing here is copied from any of them. Five guides are marked unconfirmed where the guide writers have not pinned the method down yet.
